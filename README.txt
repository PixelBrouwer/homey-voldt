Take control of charging through your home network. Sign in once with the account from your Voldt mobile app; the local key and charging functions are retrieved automatically. After pairing, Homey controls and monitors the cable locally, without keeping your password.

Use your existing Homey routines to plan charging and follow power and energy in Homey Energy and Insights. Available measurements depend on your cable's firmware. This is an independent community app by PixelBrouwer, not an official Voldt app. Wi-Fi cables with compatible local control are required.
